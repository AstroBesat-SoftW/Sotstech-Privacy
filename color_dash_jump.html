Information Collection and Use

As the developer of Color Dash, I do not independently collect, store, process, or transmit any of your personal data.

Offline Play: Color Dash is designed to be fully functional without an internet connection. When you play the game offline, absolutely zero data is collected or shared. Your gameplay, level progress, and high scores remain entirely on your device.

Online Play & Rewarded Ads: If you choose to play with an active internet connection, the game offers a feature where you can watch a rewarded video ad to continue your run after hitting the wrong color. To provide this specific feature, the app integrates a third-party advertising service, Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve these ads, personalize them, and measure their performance. This may include:

  • Device Identifiers (e.g., IDFA): Used to deliver and measure ads on your device.
  • Usage Data: Used to track whether an ad was watched successfully so the game can grant you the "continue" reward.
  • Approximate Location: May be used by the ad network to serve regionally relevant ads.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, if AdMob attempts to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for personalized advertising, you will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission prompt before any such tracking occurs. You may allow or deny this at any time from your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying this permission does not prevent you from watching rewarded ads to continue your run — it only limits ad personalization.

Children's Privacy

Color Dash does not contain any content that is inappropriate for children, and the game itself does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, regardless of age. However, when played with an active internet connection, the app displays ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising network. Color Dash is not directed at children under the age of 9, and we do not knowingly permit children under 9 to view personalized advertising within the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and are concerned about third-party ads, you can ensure the game is played entirely offline and ad-free by turning off the device's internet connection before play.
