This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information. The core principle of Puzzle Chronicles is relaxing artistic gameplay, fair competition, and strict respect for user privacy.

Information Collection and Use

As the developer of Puzzle Chronicles, I do not independently collect, store, process, or transmit any of your personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

Offline Play: Puzzle Chronicles is designed to be fully functional without an internet connection. When you play the single-player puzzle levels offline, zero data is transmitted. Your level progress, stars, unlocked chests, coins, and theme preferences (Day & Night mode) remain stored locally on your device.

Online Matchmaking & Anonymous Session ID: If you choose to enter the "Online Mode" to compete against other players, the app does not require any account registration or sign-in. Instead, the app automatically generates a random, anonymous alphanumeric identifier (e.g., 234BDFKJ...USER) stored locally in your device's cache. This temporary ID is transmitted to our matchmaking server solely to pair you with an active opponent (or an AI bot if no player is available) and synchronize puzzle progress during the match. Clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app permanently removes this identifier.

Safe In-Game Reactions (No Free-Form Chat): During online matches, players can only send pre-defined, family-friendly emojis and fixed greetings (such as "Hello!" or "Good luck!"). There is no free-text chat or user-generated content (UGC) system, ensuring that no personal information can be shared or exposed between players.

Rewarded Ads & Third-Party Services: When playing with an active internet connection, you may choose to watch rewarded video ads to enter online matches or earn bonus in-game coins and hints. To provide this feature, the app integrates a third-party advertising service, Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, personalize them, and measure their performance. This may include:

  • Device Identifiers (e.g., IDFA / Advertising ID): Used to deliver, frequency-cap, and measure ads on your device.
  • Usage & Advertising Data: Used to track ad interactions and verify whether a rewarded ad was watched completely so the game can grant your in-game reward.
  • Approximate Location (Coarse Location): Derived from your IP address by the ad network to serve regionally relevant ads.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data to ensure stability of the advertising SDK.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, if Google AdMob attempts to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for personalized advertising, you will be presented with Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission prompt before any tracking occurs. You may allow or deny this permission at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying this permission does not affect your ability to play the game or earn in-app rewards from ads—it only limits ad personalization.

Data Retention & Deletion

Since Puzzle Chronicles does not use user accounts or collect personal profiles, we do not maintain personal user records on our servers. Temporary matchmaking states are only used during active online matches. You can reset all your local game data and your anonymous session ID at any time simply by clearing the app's storage cache or uninstalling the application from your device.

Children's Privacy

Puzzle Chronicles features family-friendly artistic illustrations and does not contain any inappropriate content. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children. When played online, ads are served via Google AdMob. If you are a parent or guardian and prefer an entirely offline, ad-free environment for your child, you can disable the device's internet connection for the app before play.
